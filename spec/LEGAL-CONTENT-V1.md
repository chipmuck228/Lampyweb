Production strict upload deployment has NOT been verified, so the website does not claim all server photos pass strict validation. No app rebuild, API/schema deployment or App Privacy submission is performed by this web PR.

Key boundaries:
- Private library stays on device by default; only selected family content is uploaded. Account sign-in does not synchronize private data.
- Apple subject/internal ID/nickname/session, family social graph, shared text/photos/audio/album data, reports/blocks/moderation, raw IP security buckets are explained. No marketing/tracking purpose asserted.
- Withdrawal stops authorized reading but is not file deletion; account purge/retry and dissolved-family 30-day cleanup are separate. No global age expiry is claimed for all rate/report/audit records.
- App-managed backup seven-day and system log thirty-day/capacity configurations are described separately from cloud/manual copies. No physical-erasure guarantee or user-recovery service.
- Historical photos can retain metadata. No blanket no-GPS or metadata-free claim. On-device originals are preserved.
- Camera/photos/microphone and system Face ID/passcode, one-minute new recordings, free preview, no purchases/export/print are consistent with 1.0.0.
- In-app account deletion and report/block routes, daily report handling, support/appeal email, safe identity verification and minimum email contents are provided.
- Website hosting/network processing and the actual lampy-locale cookie are disclosed, rather than a false no-data/no-cookie statement.

The developer's legal name/address, hosting processing locations, email provider arrangements and any market-specific legal notices must reflect verified operator information before final store submission. This change does not invent a company, address, jurisdiction, fixed support SLA, unverified localization promise, broad liability waiver or regulatory approval. It is not a certification of worldwide legal compliance. The developer is identified by product/operator role and the confirmed support mailbox until exact legal identity details are supplied.
